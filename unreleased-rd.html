Ownership & Scope (NDA Compliant)

Selected to spearhead high-impact mechanical and thermal sub-assemblies for unannounced next-generation platforms. Due to strict non-disclosure obligations, specific product dimensions, performance metrics, and proprietary mechanisms are omitted.

My primary technical ownership centered on two core domains: precision optomechanics and high-efficiency air handling.

Precision Optomechanical Packaging

High-precision optical engines require sub-micron stability under dynamic vibration and thermal fluctuation. Key contributions included:

  • 01
    Kinematic Mounting & Degree-of-Freedom Constraints: Designed exact-constraint kinematic mounts (Kelvin clamps / groove-ball interfaces) preventing mechanical over-constraint and eliminating optical distortion from structural chassis flexing.
  • 02
    Athermalization & Thermal Drift Mitigation: Modeled coefficient of thermal expansion (CTE) mismatches across optical substrates, adhesives, and aluminum chassis structures. Implemented flexure geometries that absorb expansion while maintaining optical bore-sight alignment.
  • 03
    Sub-Milliradian Adjustment Mechanisms: Engineered fine-pitch kinematic adjustment hardware providing precise multi-axis alignment during manufacturing calibration while locking rigidly in place for transit.

Aerodynamic Air Handling & Particulate Control

Maintaining optical clarity and consistent internal atmospheric conditions required advanced aerodynamic flow routing:

  • 01
    CFD-Guided Duct Geometry: Shaped intake and exhaust manifolds using computational fluid dynamics (CFD) to prevent recirculation dead zones, reduce acoustic noise, and maintain uniform laminar flow across sensitive optical faces.
  • 02
    Positive-Pressure Clean Zone: Designed a cascaded pressure barrier ensuring any internal leakage flows outward, preventing external dust or aerosol ingress from contaminating the optical path.
  • 03
    Compact Blower Integration & Vibration Isolation: Designed custom silicone isolator mounts for high-static-pressure blowers, attenuating motor harmonics and preventing micro-vibrations from coupling into optical beams.
