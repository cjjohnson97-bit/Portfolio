The Kinematic Challenge & Gearbox Architecture

The mechanical goal was to engineer a pocket-sized dual-player life counter (tracking 00 to 99 for both players) with crisp mechanical detents, zero gear slip, and a satisfying tactile click.

Standard spur gear arrangements in small form factors frequently suffer from tooth binding or excessive backlash. To overcome this, I developed a compound planetary reduction train:

  • 01
    Custom Involute Tooth Geometry: Modified the standard 20° pressure angle to 25° on the 0.5 module pinions, increasing tooth root strength and preventing tip interference under micro-scale 3D printing tolerances.
  • 02
    Backlash Mitigation: Engineered integrated spring-loaded compliance directly into the idler carrier to maintain continuous tooth pitch contact without introducing binding friction.
  • 03
    Positive-Click Detent Wheel: Designed an internal scalloped cam ring with dual cantilever leaf springs, ensuring positive dial locking at every discrete integer step.

Manufacturing, Tolerances & Assembly

Prototyped and validated across both stereolithography (SLA Tough 2000 / Rigid 4000) and precision FDM setups. Applied GD&T running fit tolerances (±0.05mm) across axle shafts and snap-fit retention caps, eliminating the need for complex internal fasteners.
