Mentorship Philosophy & Culture of Excellence

Since 2018, I have served as a core mechanical design mentor for FRC Team 4909, transforming high school students into confident, analytical problem-solvers. My mentorship approach emphasizes first-principles mechanical engineering, collaborative design reviews, and empowering student ownership:

  • 01
    CAD Best Practices & Onshape Collaboration: Established industry-standard parametric modeling workflows, multi-document assembly structures, and strict revision control across 30+ student contributors.
  • 02
    Rapid Prototyping & Iteration Velocity: Taught fast wood/polycarbonate mockups to prove game piece geometry and intake compression within the first 72 hours of season kickoff.
  • 03
    Strategic Fabrication & DFM: Guided students on CNC router fixturing, tube laser fabrication, 3D printed structural pulleys/housings, and precision sheet metal stamping.

Mechanism Engineering & Subsystem Coaching

Each season introduces a new high-speed sport requiring specialized robotic kinematics:

  • 01
    Field-Centric Swerve Drivetrains: Coached the integration and optimization of 4-wheel independent swerve modules capable of 18 ft/sec rapid vectoring and high-impact defensive collisions.
  • 02
    High-Speed Game Piece Shooters & Flywheels: Applied projectile physics and MOI (Moment of Inertia) tuning to maintain consistent backspin and trajectory across variable field distances.
  • 03
    Kinematic Arms, Elevators & Multi-Stage Cascades: Engineered continuous-rigging cable elevators, constant-force spring counterbalancing, and backlash-free planetary gearboxes for sub-second endgame climbs.
