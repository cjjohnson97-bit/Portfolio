06 FIRST Robotics

CAN Bus Fault Isolator

Keeping a robot running through a broken CAN wire

One broken CAN wire takes down every motor on an FRC robot. This board detects the break, splits and terminates the bus before the break, and flags where the problem is. ESP32 with two TCAN4550 CAN-FD controllers and a relay-switched termination.
